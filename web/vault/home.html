BUILT AROUND YOUR DEVICES

Stored in the cloud.
Used on your terms.

Your browser encrypts your private identities before they’re saved. When you approve a request, the identity goes encrypted to a device you control.

Grexie’s service handles storage and delivery. It doesn’t receive your private keys in readable form or run the signer.
